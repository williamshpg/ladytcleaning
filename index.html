How much does house cleaning cost in Benin City? ▾

House cleaning costs in Benin City depend on the size of your property (number of bedrooms/bathrooms) and whether you need routine maintenance or intensive deep cleaning. What is the difference between standard cleaning and deep cleaning? ▾

Standard cleaning is routine upkeep: sweeping, mopping, surface dusting, bed making, and regular bathroom/kitchen wipe-downs. Deep cleaning is an exhaustive top-to-bottom sanitization that addresses grime buildup: tile grout scrubbing, degreasing oven and cooker hoods, cleaning inside cabinets, moving heavy appliances, descaling shower heads, and detailed baseboard extraction.

What is included in post construction cleaning in Benin City? ▾

Our post-construction and after-builder cleaning protocol includes industrial HEPA extraction of microscopic silica and plaster dust from walls, ceiling vents, and corners, careful scraping of paint and cement splatters from window glass and imported tiles, sanitization of brand-new cabinetry, and a final marble polish before furniture handover.

Look for three essential factors: (1) Verified local presence with an active Google Business Profile and verifiable client reviews, (2) Vetted, background-checked specialists with full insurance coverage, and (3) Clear satisfaction guarantees.
